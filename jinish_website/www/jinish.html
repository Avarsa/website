Systems in use

EOLS

Excise Operations and Licensing System

Used by the Excise Department across all three districts: Daman, Diu and Dadra & Nagar Haveli.

  • 100,000+ permits generated
  • 1,000+ active licences
  • ₹410 crore+ in excise revenue
Production & trade
Distilleries and breweries need approval to move stock during production. EOLS handles wholesale and industrial imports, exports, and supply to bars, wine shops and hotels.
Licensing & fees
Businesses register labels and renew licences through EOLS, which calculates and collects duties and fees.
Checks & oversight
Border guards verify permits by QR code and log crossings. EOLS supports reviews and approvals from station officers to the commissioner.
